OLamma brings people together

Stay close to the people you love

OLamma is a social network where you share what's on your mind, follow friends, and keep up with the people who matter to you, all in one calm, friendly place.

Why "OLamma"?

The name says what the app is about and how it looks.

O

for Olive, the green that gives the app its look and its calm feel.

Lamma

an Arabic word for a gathering: friends and family getting together.

The idea behind the project

Social networks are meant to bring people closer, but many of them feel crowded and noisy. OLamma is built around the simple idea of a social network that works the way people already know, with a feed, profiles, likes, comments and shares, and a design that keeps your attention on the people and posts you care about.

Anyone can create an account, post a thought, see what friends are sharing, and find new people to follow. The goal is simple: make it easy to be close to the people you love, wherever they are.

What you can do on OLamma

Share posts

Write what's on your mind, add photos or videos, and share a feeling or activity.

Choose who sees it

Set each post as public, followers only, or only me, so you stay in control of your audience.

Like, comment and share

React to your friends' posts, join the conversation, and pass posts along.

Find and follow friends

Get suggestions of people you may know, see mutual friends, and follow in one tap.

Save posts for later

Keep the posts you like in your Saved list and come back to them anytime.

Join the community

Browse the Community page to see what everyone is talking about.

What we built

OLamma is a complete social web app. The front end is built with Angular, with its own olive design: a top navigation bar, a shortcuts menu, the main feed, and a suggested-friends panel. It talks to a back-end API for accounts, posts, comments, follows and notifications.

  • A clean, readable design with one color family.
  • A layout that adapts to desktop, tablet and phone.
  • Fast, simple actions: post, like, follow and save in a click or two.
